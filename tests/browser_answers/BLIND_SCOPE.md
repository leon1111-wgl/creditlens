# CreditLens demo: what the in-tab question box covers (scope for the blind set)

The demo holds saved evidence from two official annual reports. A visitor types a question in Chinese
(sometimes English or mixed) and the tab answers from the saved figures, or says what is missing.

## Documents and issuers
- Microsoft Corporation (MSFT), Form 10-K for fiscal year 2025. Fiscal years end on June 30.
  FY2025 = 2024-07-01 to 2025-06-30. FY2024 = 2023-07-01 to 2024-06-30.
- Apple Inc. (AAPL), Form 10-K for fiscal year 2025. 52/53-week fiscal years ending late September.
  FY2025 = 2024-09-29 to 2025-09-27 (52 weeks). FY2024 = 2023-10-01 to 2024-09-28 (52 weeks).
- Each report's statements print the current year and the prior year (the income and cash-flow statements also print
  an older third year, which is NOT registered in the demo).

## Registered periods
- FY2024 and FY2025 only, annual figures only. Balance-sheet items are as of each fiscal year end
  (MSFT 2024-06-30 / 2025-06-30; AAPL 2024-09-28 / 2025-09-27).
- Not registered: FY2023 or earlier, FY2026, any quarter or half-year, calendar-year (Jan-Dec) figures, trailing twelve months.

## Registered metrics (both issuers, both years, unless noted)
All are consolidated statement lines, as printed, in USD millions.
1. Total revenue (MSFT "Total revenue"; AAPL "Total net sales")
2. Product revenue (MSFT "Product"; AAPL "Products")
3. Service revenue (MSFT "Service and other"; AAPL "Services")
4. Total cost of revenue (MSFT "Total cost of revenue"; AAPL "Total cost of sales")
5. Product cost of revenue; 6. Service cost of revenue
7. Gross margin amount (gross profit)
8. Gross margin percentage (gross margin / revenue), total, product and service — a registered calculation
9. Net income
10. Cash from operating activities (MSFT "Net cash from operations"; AAPL "Cash generated by operating activities")
11. Payments for property, plant and equipment (capex as the PPE cash-payment line only; not all capital investment)
12. Short-term debt (MSFT "Short-term debt"; AAPL "Commercial paper")
13. Current portion of long-term debt (MSFT "Current portion of long-term debt"; AAPL current "Term debt")
14. Long-term debt, non-current (MSFT "Long-term debt"; AAPL non-current "Term debt")
15. Total current assets; 16. Total current liabilities
17. MSFT only: total of cash, cash equivalents and short-term investments (one combined line; cash and cash equivalents alone is NOT registered for MSFT)
18. AAPL only: cash and cash equivalents; current marketable securities (two separate lines)

## What the box can do
- Look up a metric for an issuer and fiscal year (the answer names the page, the box on the page, the printed unit and an OCR check outcome).
- Compare the two fiscal years of one issuer (change and growth rate), after checking that the two figures are comparable.
- Say whether two figures are comparable, and why not when they are not. Microsoft vs Apple figures are shown side by side only:
  the two are not a registered peer benchmark, their fiscal years cover different dates, and their calendars differ, so the
  demo never ranks them or computes a difference between them.
- Convert the printed USD-million figure exactly into another USD scale the visitor asks for (e.g. 亿美元, billion, 万美元).

## What it must refuse, naming what is missing
- Any other company, subsidiary, segment (e.g. Azure, iPhone, Greater China), parent-only (母公司) statements.
- Any period not registered (see above), relative periods that cannot be pinned to a fiscal year.
- Any metric not listed (e.g. operating income, EBITDA, EPS, R&D, total assets, equity, free cash flow, net debt, ratios other than gross margin %).
- Conversion into another currency (RMB 元/人民币, HKD, EUR...): no exchange rate is registered.
- Ranking or computing a difference between Microsoft and Apple.
